# Cookies on the Petitions Service

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you use a website. This service uses cookies for its session and to remember your choices about optional analytics.

## Essential cookies

The service uses **_epets_session** to maintain a secure browser session, including access to restricted staff functions. This cookie is set by the Rails application with Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict attributes when a session is established. Disabling essential cookies may prevent some parts of the service from working.

## Cookie preferences

The cookie settings control records whether you have chosen to allow or reject additional analytics cookies. The current frontend uses **uk-parliament.cookie-policy** (a retained identifier from the original UK software) and **petition-cookie-policy** to store this choice. The preference cookie is configured to expire after 365 days.

An additional analytics preference is **off by default** until you actively choose to enable it. Accepting an optional cookie is not required to read public petitions. A preference does not itself establish that a separate analytics provider is operating.

## Change your preferences

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer, or the cookie banner when it appears, to change your choice. You can also delete cookies for this site in your browser's settings. Removing cookies may cause the banner to appear again.

This information describes the cookies used by the current Petitions Service. For questions about information collected by the service, read the [privacy information](/privacy) or [contact Parliament](https://parliament.uhrblx.com/contact/).
