# Privacy information for the Petitions Service

This information explains how the current United Hampshire Parliament Petitions Service handles information when you browse the website, use the feedback form or access restricted parliamentary staff functions.

## Who is responsible

The House of Commons Petitions Service operates as part of the parliamentary administration. It is separate from the United Hampshire Government's GOV.UH publishing service. You can [contact Parliament](https://parliament.uhrblx.com/contact/) about personal information relating to the service.

## Information collected

When you browse this site, the service may process technical request information, including IP addresses, browser information and session cookies, for delivery, security, troubleshooting and recordkeeping.

The [feedback form](/feedback) asks for a comment and may include your email address and the relevant petition title or link. The application also records the request's IP address and browser user-agent. These records are held as restricted service data and are not automatically displayed as a public petition.

Restricted parliamentary staff access uses staff account records, password hashes, encrypted authenticator information and security-related events. Those records are not part of the public petitions record.

## What is not currently open

The electronic petitions scheme is currently closed to new submissions and signatures. This service is not currently collecting new public petition signatures or supporters through the normal application process. Any future opening must be accompanied by the House's approved petition rules and an appropriate review of the published information about the additional personal data collected.

## How information is used

Service information is used for operating and protecting the website, responding to appropriate enquiries, keeping administrative records and administering any authorised parliamentary work. Public petition information and restricted identity or contact details must be handled separately.

Cookie choices and essential session cookies are explained in the [cookie information](/cookies). The site must not publish a person's private feedback or identity-validation details simply because they contact the service.

## Contact, correction and retention questions

For an enquiry about your personal information, access or correction, retention, permitted disclosure, or a privacy complaint, [contact the House Administration](https://parliament.uhrblx.com/contact/) and identify the Petitions Service. The applicable legal requirements and retention schedules govern how the request is considered. No fixed retention period is claimed here without an authoritative schedule.

This page describes the current operation of the service; it does not authorise any future petition collection or replace a parliamentary decision about the scheme.
