# Petition standards

The United Hampshire House of Commons determines the rules for electronic petitions. The administrative Petitions Service cannot set admissibility requirements or signature thresholds on its own.

**Electronic petitions are not currently open for submission or signature.** The House has not yet brought an electronic petitions scheme into operation with the necessary published rules. This page therefore does not impose new criteria or confer a right to create a petition.

## What an authorised scheme must cover

The House's rules will determine who may submit or support a petition, the information needed to validate a petition or signature, when it may be published, and how related or duplicate petitions are managed.

The requested action must be considered against the responsibilities of the body to which the petition is addressed. Matters for England, Havenstead, local authorities, an independent body or another institution must be distinguished from matters within the responsibility of the United Hampshire Government or the House of Commons.

Where an authorised scheme provides for rejection or a request for changes, staff must explain the relevant rule and administrative outcome. A petition must not be refused merely because its content is critical of the Government or Parliament.

## Support and parliamentary consideration

The House must separately determine any supporter requirements, length of time open for signatures, Government-response criteria, consideration for debate and related consequences. **No numerical thresholds are stated or implied by this page.** A petition's appearance on a website, or the number of people supporting it, cannot of itself compel legislation or a debate.
