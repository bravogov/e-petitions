# Accessibility of the Petitions Service

This statement concerns the United Hampshire Parliament Petitions Service at petition.parliament.uhrblx.com, including its public pages and parliamentary staff sign-in.

We aim to make parliamentary information accessible and to follow the relevant Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2 AA) when developing and reviewing the service.

## Using the website

The public pages include links, headings, text and forms. You may be able to adjust text size, zoom and display settings in your browser and use keyboard navigation or assistive technology. The service has not yet completed a verified full WCAG 2.2 AA audit, so we cannot certify that every function meets the standard.

Electronic petition creation and signature collection are currently disabled. This is a service-authorisation restriction, not an accessibility setting.

## Report a problem or request assistance

If you cannot use part of this service, need information in an alternative accessible format or encounter a problem with keyboard navigation, screen reading or a form, [contact Parliament](https://parliament.uhrblx.com/contact/). Describe the affected page and the assistance you need. You do not need to disclose a medical condition simply to report an accessibility issue.

Questions about an authorised written or assisted petition route should also be directed to the House Administration, because the eligibility and admissibility requirements must be set by the House.
